Approved. The added cases cover ordinary Go standard-library imports without blanket fallback, a real Go fuzz-corpus input named README whose change turns the full Go suite from passing to failing, unresolved Python import provenance, and a Node-to-Python subprocess dependency requiring impact or full fallback. The fuzz case asserts the owner test is affected or full fallback is required, and verifies the full runner's actual failure rather than relying on a fabricated expectation. It explicitly skips when Go 1.22 or newer is unavailable; release verification of that case therefore requires an environment with Go installed.

At checkpoint b97c026 the four added cases took 5.6 seconds with Go 1.24.9: the fuzz fixture independently passed before and failed after the seed change, while its impact assertion and two other new cases remained red. The four targeted suites took 104 seconds in total; three passed and the conditional suite retained three failures. The extra cost is proportionate to running a real Go suite twice, and the measurements are not evidence of a completed implementation.
