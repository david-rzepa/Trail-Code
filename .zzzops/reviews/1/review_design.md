# Re-review: conditional schema v2

The revised design makes schema v2 opt-in, preserves conservative schema v1, and requires consumers to validate every model and context requirement against independently obtained evidence before using sparse impact. Unknown requirements or unbounded gaps lead to full fallback or explicit failure. Its initial Node/TypeScript model uses whole-file import/export/require closure and explicitly treats unresolved dynamic loading, registration and external input relationships as gaps; its acceptance scenario requires two independently traceable test targets and a compatible baseline, plus broadening or inclusion for dynamic, fixture and import-effect cases. Report compatibility, model and environment changes, and v1-only consumers are covered. The recorded owner approval is cited in the design. No new material finding in this change.
